Adélie

Adélie penguins are bold little explorers that travel across sea ice to hunt. They often slide on their bellies to save energy.

Chinstrap

Chinstrap penguins are very social and live in huge, loud groups. They are strong climbers and can nest on steep, rocky places.

Gentoo

Gentoo penguins are some of the fastest penguin swimmers. They often search for food closer to shore than other penguins.
